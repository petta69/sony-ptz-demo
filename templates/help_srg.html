HELP SRG



The HELP SRG (Shared Resource Group) is a collaborative initiative designed to facilitate the sharing of resources, knowledge, and expertise among members of the HELP community. The primary goal of the HELP SRG is to foster a supportive environment where individuals can access and contribute to a wide range of resources that can aid in their personal and professional development.

Prepare the Camera


To prepare the camera for use, follow these steps:

  1. Locate the DIP switch on the camera. It is usually found on the back or bottom of the device.
  2. Refer to the camera's user manual to identify the specific settings for the DIP switch. Each switch may have a different function, such as enabling or disabling certain features, adjusting the camera's behavior, or configuring network settings.
  3. Use a small tool, such as a pen or a paperclip, to toggle the switches to the desired positions based on the instructions provided in the user manual.
  4. >Once you have set the DIP switches according to your requirements, power on the camera and verify that it is functioning correctly. You may need to consult the user manual for any additional configuration steps or troubleshooting tips related to the DIP switch settings.


Factory Reset


In order to perform a factory reset, follow these steps:

  1. Locate the reset button on the back of the device. Number 8 on the picture.
  2. Use a small, pointed object (like a paperclip) to press and hold the reset button.
  3. Hold the button for approximately 10 seconds or longer.
  4. Release the reset button and allow the device to complete the reset process. This may take a few minutes.
  5. Once the reset is complete, the device will be listed again in the device list.
