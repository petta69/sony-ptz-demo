Prepare the Camera



To prepare the camera for use, follow these steps:

  1. Locate the DIP switch on the camera. It is found on the back of the camera.
  2. You will now need to activate VISCA. Use a small tool, such as a pen or a paperclip, to toggle DIP switch number 3 to it's "ON" position.
  3. In order to cotnrol output selection from webUI you will also need to set DIP switch number 1 to it's "ON" position.
  4. Once you have set the DIP switches you MUST perform a power cycle in order to make new settings available.


Factory Reset


In order to perform a factory reset, follow these steps:

  1. Locate the reset button on the back of the device. Number 8 on the picture.
  2. Use a small, pointed object (like a paperclip) to press and hold the reset button.
  3. Hold the button for approximately 10 seconds or longer.
  4. Release the reset button and allow the device to complete the reset process. This may take a few minutes.
  5. Once the reset is complete, the device will be listed again in the device list.
  6. After the camera is listed you can now click on the link for it. First time you log in you will have to use "admin" and username and leave password blank.
  7. You will then need to set a password for your camera. Please use "Sony123456" as the password.


Camera Settings


In order to make the Camera to work with Companion you will need to turn off the "Referer check" in the settings menu:


After you have turned off the "Referer check" you will need to scroll down and click on the "Apply" button in order to save the settings.



In order to adjust the video output, navigate to the "Installation" section in the "System" settings menu. Here you have the "Video out" selection.


After you have selected the correct "Format" you will need to scroll down and click on the "Apply" button in order to save the settings.
