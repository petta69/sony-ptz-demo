Companion Menu

On the left hand side you see the menu for Companion. There are two main items you will need to keep track on:

  1. Connections
  2. Buttons


View/Add/Edit connections

In order to view, add or edit connections you will need to click on the "Connections" menu item. Here you will see a list of all connections that are currently configured. You can also add new connections by clicking on the "Add Connection" button. To edit an existing connection,

To edit a connection you simply click on the connection you want to edit. You will then be presented with a screen that allows you to edit the connection settings. Once you have made your changes, click on the "Save" button to save your changes.
